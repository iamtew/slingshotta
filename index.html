Slingshotta

Graze a planet's ring for one speed boost. Aim too low, but not into the body, and you bounce off slower. Hit the body and you start over.

The blue arrow is your path. The nose is only where you point. Turn, then burn, or nudge the path with the translation thrusters.

Click the view for the mouse. Mouse or arrows pitch and yaw. Q / E roll. W / S main engine. A / D strafe. Space / C up and down.

Gamepad: right stick aims, left stick strafes and thrusts, triggers throttle, bumpers roll.

Enter or Esc opens and closes this menu.

A spin keeps going until you counter it or it eases off. Stop the spin before you line up a burn.

Doing nothing at the start misses Vela. Steer into a ring when you want the boost. The belt rocks are solid.
