Graze a body's instrument ring for one speed boost. Hit the body and the Pilot crashes. Rocks and moons pull a little too.

        
Point the nose and the ship goes that way. The heat-shield nose is heading. The dashed cyan line is a ballistic read of current velocity. The round disc is a 3D radar.

Move the mouse to turn. Stop the mouse to stop turning. Brake with S: the hull pitches and the bell burns; let go and it finishes the turn. In a well the hull leans in and the path follows, and that curve stays on the way out. Do not reverse.

        
Doing nothing at the start misses Vela. Steer into the rings when the Pilot wants the boost. Belt rocks are solid and have a thin well.

Molniya

            
Yuzhnoye Type 7 · hull 17. One seat, one bell.

            
    
              
  • Heat shield. Lotos silica-phenolic cap, 38 mm. One 11 km/s graze. It is meant to char.
    • 
              
  • Tanks. Two spheres, the aft one stretched. UDMH / N2O4, helium at 220 bar. Hypergolic if they meet.
    • 
              
  • RCS. Eight pods, sixteen nitrogen jets. A jet fires only when its torque matches the spin.
    • 
              
  • Main engine. Khimavtomatika K-17, copper throat, 180 kN in vacuum. Cruise is a throttle cap. Boost opens it. Brake turns the hull so this bell burns.
