Slingshotta

Graze a planet's haze for one speed boost. Hit the body and you crash.

Point the nose and the ship goes that way. The blue arrow is a path read. The round disc is a 3D radar.

  • Click the view to capture the mouse
  • Mouse / arrows: pitch and yaw
  • A / D or Q / E: roll
  • W: thrust
  • S: brake
  • Shift: boost
  • Gamepad sticks: aim
  • Triggers: thrust / brake
  • B: boost
  • Bumpers: roll
  • Enter / Esc: menu

Move the mouse to turn. Stop the mouse to stop turning. Brake with S, do not reverse.

Doing nothing at the start misses Vela. Steer into the haze when you want the boost. The belt rocks are solid.

Enter or Esc to close
