# Slingshotta

Fly a ship past planets. Graze the ring for a boost. Hit the body and you crash.

## Play

From this folder:

```
npx serve .
```

Open the URL it prints. Click the view to capture the mouse. Enter or Esc opens the menu.

The ship flies where the nose points. W thrusts, S brakes, Shift boosts. Mouse or arrows pitch and yaw. A / D rolls. The white tick on the nose is heading. The dashed cyan line is where current velocity goes. The round disc is a 3D radar. Graze the instrument rings around a body. Rocks have a small well too. Distances and speeds are kilometres.

A gamepad works too: either stick aims, triggers thrust and brake, B boosts, bumpers roll.
