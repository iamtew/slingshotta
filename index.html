Slingshotta

Graze a body's instrument ring for one speed boost. Hit the body and you crash. Rocks and moons pull a little too.

Point the nose and the ship goes that way. The white tick is heading. The dashed cyan line is a ballistic read of current velocity. The round disc is a 3D radar.

  • Click the view to capture the mouse
  • Mouse / arrows: pitch and yaw
  • A / D or Q / E: roll
  • W: thrust
  • S: brake (hull pitches, bell burns; release finishes the flip)
  • Shift: boost
  • Gamepad sticks: aim
  • Triggers: thrust / brake
  • B: boost
  • Bumpers: roll
  • Touch overlays: thrust, brake, boost, roll, look disc
  • Gyro (if on): tilt aims pitch and yaw
  • Enter / Esc / Start / Menu overlay: menu
  • D-pad or stick: menu move
  • A: menu select, B: back

Move the mouse to turn. Stop the mouse to stop turning. Brake with S: the hull pitches and the bell burns; let go and it finishes the turn. In a well the hull leans in and the path follows, and that curve stays on the way out. Do not reverse.

Doing nothing at the start misses Vela. Steer into the rings when you want the boost. Belt rocks are solid and have a thin well.
