Graze a body's instrument ring for one speed boost. Hit the body and the Pilot crashes. Rocks and moons pull a little too. Belt rocks are solid and have a thin well.

        
Point the nose. Heat-shield is heading. Dashed cyan is current velocity. Radar is the round disc. The G ball sits beside speed and slides off center under load; a hard slingshot darkens the view.

        
Play is Free roam, Daily Race, or Weekly Race. Free roam and Weekly Race share this week's seed. Daily Race is today's. Race is graze the listed worlds, moons, and asteroids in order. Time plus ribbon miss is the score. A typed seed lives on Developer.

        
Mouse turn, stop the mouse to stop turning. S brake: hull pitches, bell burns; let go and it finishes the turn. In a well the hull leans in and that curve stays on the way out. Do not reverse. Spawn coasts past the first well; steer in for the ring. A race clock starts after 3-2-1-GO.

        
      

      

        
Fly. Click the view to capture the mouse. W thrust. S brake. Shift boost. Mouse or arrows pitch and yaw. A / D or Q / E roll. Gamepad: sticks aim, triggers thrust / brake, B boost, bumpers roll. Touch pads if shown. Gyro (if on): tilt pitch and yaw.

        
Pause. Enter / Esc / Start / Menu overlay. Arrows or D-pad move (hold repeats). Enter or A selects. Esc or B backs. Left / right cycles a toggle. Right stick on Ship looks around the hull.
